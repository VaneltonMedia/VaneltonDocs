# Privacy Policy - Vanelton Media

**Last updated:** 01/05/2025

Hi there! Welcome to Vanelton Media. We believe in being open and clear about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains how Vanelton Media & Software LTDA (the company we're registering, which we'll call "Vanelton Media," "we," "us," or "our") and our different teams (or divisions) collect, use, share, and look after your personal information when you use any of our stuff – like our websites, apps, games, and other products (we'll call these the "Services").

A core part of our Services is the **Vanelton ID**, our universal account system. Your Vanelton ID lets you access various Vanelton Media services with a single login.

This policy is for everyone who uses our Services, including the Vanelton ID system.

**Quick Heads-Up:** By using our Services (including creating or using a Vanelton ID), you're telling us you're okay with how we collect and use your info as described here. We get it if you don't agree with every single thing. However, if you choose not to agree, some parts of our Services might not work for you, or you might not be able to use them at all. Just wanted to be upfront about that!

## 1. What Information We Collect

To make our Services work and keep improving them, we need to collect some information. Here’s a breakdown:

### a) Info You Give Us Directly (Your Vanelton ID & More):

* **Vanelton ID / Account Details:** When you sign up for a Vanelton ID (our universal account), we'll ask for things like your name, email, birthday, language, phone number, and a password. This ID is your key to many of our Services.
* **Profile Info:** Want to make your profile more *you*? You can add things like a profile picture, nickname, your interests, and what you like or don't like to your Vanelton ID profile. This is usually optional.
* **Payment Details:** If you buy something or subscribe (perhaps through a service linked to your Vanelton ID), we'll need payment info (like credit card numbers). Don't worry, this is handled by secure payment partners, not stored directly by us in most cases.
* **Your Preferences:** We keep track of your settings and preferences linked to your Vanelton ID, including content choices and ad settings, so we can make things more relevant across different Services.
* **When You Talk to Us:** If you contact our support team (maybe about your Vanelton ID or another service), we'll have the info you share in that conversation (like your name, email, phone number, and the message itself).

### b) Info We Collect Automatically:

When you use our Services (often while logged in with your Vanelton ID), some info gets collected automatically by our systems:

* **Device Info:** We learn about the device you're using – like the model (is it an iPhone? A Samsung? A PC?), the operating system (iOS, Android, Windows?), unique IDs for that device, and network details.
* **How You Connect & Use Things:** We log details about how you use our Services. This includes your IP address (a sort of online address for your device), browser type, internet provider, which pages you visited before and after ours, what you looked at on our site, your device's operating system, timestamps, and basically what you clicked on and browsed across the services linked to your Vanelton ID.
* **Where You Are (Location):** If you give us permission, we might check your device's location. This helps us offer features specific to your area or show more relevant ads. You can usually turn this off in your device settings.
* **Cookies & Tracking Stuff:** We use "cookies" (tiny files stored on your device) and similar tech (like pixels or beacons). These help us see how you use our Services, remember your preferences (like keeping you logged into your Vanelton ID), analyze trends, manage the site, and understand our users better. This includes tracking your browsing on our Services and how you interact with ads. You can usually control cookies in your browser settings.

### c) Info We Get From Others:

Sometimes, we get information about you from other places:

* **Partners & Data Providers:** We might get info from business partners or data analysis companies (like general demographic info or interests) to help us understand our users better.
* **Public Sources:** Sometimes information is publicly available.
* **Social Media:** If you link a social media account to our Services or Vanelton ID, we might get info from that platform according to their rules and your settings.
* **Third-Party Developers:** If you use your Vanelton ID to log into an app from a certified third-party developer, they might share some usage information back with us (as outlined in their privacy policy and our developer agreements).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

So, what do we *do* with all this info? Good question! We use it to:

* **Run and Improve Our Services:** Manage your Vanelton ID, make sure everything works smoothly across different services, fix bugs, and add new cool features.
* **Enable Universal Access:** Use your Vanelton ID details to log you into different Vanelton Media services (like LaxsrAI, Volcam games, developer console) without needing separate accounts.
* **Handle Payments:** Process your purchases and manage your subscriptions linked to your Vanelton ID.
* **Make It Personal:** Show you content, features, or ads that we think you'll find interesting, based on your Vanelton ID profile and activity.
* **Understand Usage:** Analyze how people are using our Services (often aggregated or anonymized) so we can make them better and develop new things.
* **Talk to You:** Send you important updates about your Vanelton ID or Services, help you with customer support, share news, and send marketing messages (but only if you're okay with that – you can usually manage preferences in your Vanelton ID settings).
* **Support Third-Party Logins:** Allow you to use your Vanelton ID to log into approved third-party applications.
* **Stay Safe & Legal:** Find and stop fraud, security problems, or illegal activities related to Vanelton ID accounts or our Services. We also need to follow the laws and regulations that apply to us.
* **Research & Development:** Use data (often anonymized or aggregated) to improve our technology, especially our AI models.
* **Share Open Source:** For our Open Vanelton Media project, we might publish code building blocks, but we're careful not to include any personal details.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We don't sell your personal information in the traditional sense. However, we sometimes need to share it with others to make our Services work, enable features you choose to use, or for legal reasons. Here’s who we might share it with:

### a) Our Own Teams:

Information associated with your Vanelton ID might be shared between our different divisions (like Volcam Studios and LaxsrAI) so they can recognize you, provide integrated features (like shared progress or settings), and operate effectively.

### b) Service Providers:

We work with other companies that help us run our business and provide the Services (like cloud hosting providers, payment processors, data analysts, customer support tools, email services). They only get the info they need to do their job for us (often related to supporting the Vanelton ID infrastructure) and have contractual obligations to keep it safe and confidential.

### c) Third-Party Developers (Via Vanelton ID Login):

* **Your Choice:** You may have the option to use your Vanelton ID to log into applications or services offered by third-party developers who have integrated with our Vanelton Media Console.
* **What's Shared:** When you choose to log in to a third-party app using your Vanelton ID, we will ask for your permission to share certain information from your Vanelton ID profile with that developer (e.g., your name, email, profile picture, user ID). We will clearly tell you what information will be shared before you approve the connection.
* **Our Control & Certification:** We take this seriously. Developers who want to use the Vanelton ID login must go through a manual approval process with strict quality control checks by Vanelton Media. We aim to ensure they are committed to handling user data responsibly.
* **Their Responsibility:** However, **once you authorize the connection and your information is shared with the third-party developer, their use of your data is governed by *their own* privacy policy and practices.** While we certify them, Vanelton Media is not responsible for how they independently collect, use, or share your information after you've logged into their service. We encourage you to review their privacy policy before connecting your Vanelton ID.

### d) Legal Reasons:

If the law requires it (like a court order or subpoena), or if we genuinely believe it's necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request, we may have to share your info, including Vanelton ID details.

### e) Business Changes:

If Vanelton Media goes through a big change (like being bought by another company, merging, or even bankruptcy), your information (including Vanelton ID data) might be transferred as part of that deal, following legal rules.

### f) With Your Permission:

If there's another reason to share your info not covered here, we'll ask for your specific permission first.

## 4. Cookies and Tracking Stuff

As we mentioned, we use cookies and similar tech. These help our Services work better, let us understand how you use them, and keep you logged into your Vanelton ID across sessions. You generally have control over these:

* **Browser Settings:** Most web browsers let you manage cookies (block them, delete them, etc.).
* **Device Settings:** Your phone or tablet might have settings to limit tracking.
* **Vanelton ID Settings (If Applicable):** We may offer specific controls related to cookies or tracking within your Vanelton ID account settings.

Keep in mind that blocking some cookies might affect how well our Services work or require you to log in more often.

## 5. Keeping Your Data Safe

Protecting your Vanelton ID and associated information is important to us. We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures (like encryption, access controls, secure login procedures) to try and keep your data safe from unauthorized access, use, or loss.

However, please remember that **no online system is 100% secure**. While we do our best, we can't absolutely guarantee the security of your information. You also play a role by using a strong password for your Vanelton ID and keeping it confidential.

## 6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We'll only keep your personal information (including your Vanelton ID data) for as long as we need it for the reasons we described in this policy. Generally, this means we keep your Vanelton ID information as long as your account is active.

Sometimes, we might need to keep it longer if the law requires it (like for tax or accounting records) or for other legitimate reasons (like resolving disputes, enforcing our agreements, or preventing fraud).

## 7. Your Privacy Rights (Especially under LGPD)

Depending on where you live, you have certain rights regarding your personal information linked to your Vanelton ID. Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) gives Brazilians specific rights, and other laws might apply too. These often include the right to:

* **Know & Access:** Ask us what personal information we have about you (associated with your Vanelton ID) and get a copy of it.
* **Correct:** Ask us to fix any information in your Vanelton ID profile or associated data that's wrong or incomplete.
* **Delete:** Ask us to delete your Vanelton ID and associated personal information (though there are some exceptions where we might need to keep some data).
* **Take It With You (Portability):** Ask for your Vanelton ID data in a format you can easily take to another service.
* **Object:** Object to us using your information for certain purposes (like direct marketing).
* **Withdraw Consent:** If we're using your information based on your permission, you can change your mind and withdraw that permission (this might affect your ability to use certain services).

To use these rights, please get in touch using the contact details at the end of this policy, likely through your Vanelton ID account management tools or by contacting our support. We'll respond according to the law.

## 8. About Kids' Privacy

We understand it's extra important to protect kids' privacy online.

* **Vanelton ID Age:** Creating a Vanelton ID may have age restrictions depending on the region and applicable laws (like COPPA and LGPD).
* **General Services:** Most of our Services linked to Vanelton ID are intended for a general audience, not specifically for children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country). We don't intentionally collect personal info from kids on these services without parental consent where required.
* **Kid-Directed Services:** Some of our Services, like certain games from Volcam Studios, *might* be designed for or appeal to children. Access might still require a parent's Vanelton ID or follow specific procedures for children's accounts if offered.
* **Compliance:** When a Service *is* aimed at kids under the age of digital consent, we follow specific rules like COPPA and LGPD.
* **Parental Consent:** For these kid-directed services or features, we won't knowingly collect personal information (beyond what's needed for basic functions) without getting verifiable parental consent first. If we need more info, we'll ask for consent and explain what we're collecting.
* **Parents' Rights:** Parents can ask to review, change, or delete the information we have about their child linked to our services.

**Parents:** If you think your child has given us personal info (perhaps by creating a Vanelton ID underage or using a service without your okay), please contact us right away! We'll look into it and take appropriate action.

This means your Vanelton ID information might be transferred to and stored on computers outside of your jurisdiction, where data protection laws might be different.

By creating and using a Vanelton ID and our Services, you agree that your information can be transferred to Brazil and potentially other locations where we or our partners operate. We'll ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers as required by law and protect your data according to this policy.
